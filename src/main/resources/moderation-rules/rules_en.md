# House rules of {operator}

Many people work together here. To make that go well, these rules apply to everything you write, upload or share, in issues, comments, pages, files and your profile.

There is zero tolerance for content that breaks these rules and for people who harass others.

## 1. Respect

Treat everyone with respect, even when you disagree. Criticise the work, never the person.

## 2. Don't put anyone down

No insults, no bullying and no harassment. Nobody is put down for their origin, skin colour, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other characteristic.

## 3. No threats and no violence

No threats, no calls for violence and no glorifying of violence, not even hinted at or "just for fun".

## 4. No sexual content, protect children

No sexual or pornographic content. Anything that sexualises or endangers children is forbidden without exception. Moderation removes it and may report it to law enforcement.

## 5. Respect other people's privacy

Don't share other people's private data without their consent, such as addresses, phone numbers, health data, photos or messages that aren't meant for everyone.

## 6. No spam, no fraud, no malware

No advertising nobody asked for, no chain letters, no phishing, no deception and no files that cause harm.

## 7. The law and the rights of others

Follow the law. Only share what you are allowed to share, and respect copyright, trademarks and other rights of others.

## Reporting

If you see something that breaks these rules, report it in the app. A report you know to be false breaks these rules itself.

Questions are answered by the moderation of {operator} at {contact}.

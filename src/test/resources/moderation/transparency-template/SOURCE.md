# Templates of the Commission

The CSV parts of the transparency report template, Annex I of Commission Implementing
Regulation (EU) 2024/2835, as the Commission publishes them in English:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/implementing-regulation-laying-down-templates-concerning-transparency-reporting-obligations
(downloaded 2026-10-24, "CSV Part 1" to "CSV Part 11"; Parts 9 and 10 apply to very large
platforms only and are not part of Hinata's figures).

`AnnualFiguresTemplateTest` compares the head and the row keys of every file Hinata exports with
these copies. Do not edit them; replace them with a newer version of the Commission's files.
